# Proposal

## Why
The current proof has one course with chapter jumps, and its tunnel overlay dims
cars and lights together while suppressing scenery. Distinct composed review
levels will exercise reusable assets/metadata, terrain variation, conditions and
night lighting, including the user's Indiana/Iowa autumn-road memory.

## What Changes
- Q/W/E/R/T/Y select six distinct illustrative levels;1–8 retain within-level
  chapter replay. Move restart to Backspace because R now selects a level.
- Data-driven level packs select geometry seed/shape, scenery palette/materials,
  sky, weather, canopy eligibility, lamps and lighting parameters atomically.
- Retain scenery at night, illuminate the road and side objects with bounded
  steering-responsive headlight fields, and keep emissive car/lamp/signal cores
  outside night grading. Add short time-based light trails with reduced-motion
  fallback, no whole-scene temporal smearing.
- Replace disconnected central hanging arches with conditionally placed crowns
  belonging to roadside trees; compose an autumn sunset canopy with warm leaf
  transmission, dappled light and wet shoulder leaf drifts with clear tire tracks.
- Record research application/rejection, runtime swap tests and local evidence.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `review-levels`: composed proof packs and atomic selection/replay.
### Modified Capabilities
- `art`: lit night materials/emissives, bounded temporal light effects and
  coherent condition-aware canopy placement.

## Impact
Proof-only controller course configuration, renderer/asset cache, input shortcuts,
lighting helpers and tests. No native platform, production map, new cloud receipt
or ranking changes. Use existing approved art and cached material variants for
this sample, without bulk asset generation. Validate geometry/lighting formulas,
repeated pack swaps, release build and browser night/input behavior; record actual
backing pixels and timing separately from visual/user/physical-phone approval.
